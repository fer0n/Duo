# Privacy Policy for FitTracker

**Last updated: 15 August 2026**

FitTracker collects no data. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no
third-party SDKs, and makes no network requests of its own.

With your permission, the app reads step count and cycling distance from Apple
Health to show your challenge progress. This data is only read and used on your
device. You can revoke access anytime in Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.

Your settings and latest progress values are stored locally on your device so
the app, its widgets, and the Apple Watch app can display them. Deleting the app
removes this data.

Reminders, if enabled, are scheduled locally by iOS — no push server, no device
token.
